1 Introduction

Welcome to JapMala (जपमाला), a devotional mobile application designed and developed by Sundramdotdev.

We respect your spiritual practice and your privacy. We believe that your devotion, your choice of prayers, and the rhythm of your chanting are sacred and private. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles, how the mobile application operates, and how information relating to your use of JapMala is handled.

2 Information Collected

JapMala is architected around a local-first principle. The current version of the application collects and stores only the functional data necessary to provide you with a meaningful Japa experience:

  • Chant Counters: The number of chants completed in an active mala session (e.g. 1 to 108).
  • Mala Completion Statistics: Aggregate counts of completed malas, total mantra repetitions, and timestamped daily progress records.
  • App Preferences: Your chosen app language (Hindi or English), chosen mala bead type (Rudraksha or Tulsi), sound feedback toggles, and haptic vibration preferences.
  • Custom Mantra Entries: Any custom mantra names, texts, or targets you voluntarily enter into the application.
Where does this information reside?

All of the information listed above is stored locally in the private sandbox storage of your mobile device (via standard Android SharedPreferences / SQLite / internal storage). It is never transmitted to any external server maintained by Sundramdotdev.

3 Information Not Collected

To ensure complete peace of mind, JapMala deliberately avoids collecting personal identifying information:

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or email address.
  • We do not require user account registration, password creation, or social logins (Google, Apple, Facebook).
  • We do not collect or access your device location, GPS coordinates, or IP address tracking history.
  • We do not collect financial information, credit card numbers, or UPI credentials.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.

4 Local Data Storage & Security

Because your data resides on your physical phone, the security of your chanting data is protected by your device's native operating system security model. Android assigns a unique user ID (UID) to the application sandbox, preventing other applications from reading JapMala's internal database or preference files without system-level device rooting or physical device access.

We recommend keeping your mobile operating system updated and maintaining a screen lock (PIN, biometric, or password) on your personal smartphone to protect all local app data.

5 Japa History and Custom Mantras

Your spiritual history—including which deity you chant to, how many malas you complete each day, and what custom prayers you save—is treated as confidential. We hold the conviction that prayer records belong solely to the devotee. Sundramdotdev has no visibility into your chanting counts or your spiritual discipline.

6 Offline Functionality & Internet Connectivity

JapMala is fully functional without an internet connection. You can use the digital mala in remote pilgrimage locations, on flights, inside temple sanctums, or during periods when your mobile device is in Airplane Mode.

The application does not periodically dial home to verify licenses, stream media, or report usage telemetry.

7 Third-Party Services & SDKs

The core JapMala application does not bundle third-party commercial advertising networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network) or cross-site tracking analytics.

If you download JapMala through third-party distribution channels (such as APKPure, GitHub Releases, or app stores), please note that those platforms may independently collect platform analytics (such as download counts, operating system versions, and crash reports) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

8 App Permissions

JapMala requests only minimal, non-sensitive Android permissions necessary to deliver the devotional features:

  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide subtle haptic vibration pulses when advancing beads and upon completing a full 108-bead mala. This can be toggled on or off in App Settings.
  • WAKE_LOCK (Optional): Allows the screen to remain gently illuminated during continuous meditation if enabled in user preferences.

9 Data Backup, Portability, and Deletion

You maintain total control over your chanting history:

  • Reset History: You can reset or clear your chanting statistics directly from the App Settings screen at any time.
  • Complete Deletion: Uninstalling the JapMala app from your device automatically purges all locally stored preferences, mantras, and counter databases immediately via the standard Android application lifecycle.

10 Children's Privacy

JapMala is suitable for users of all ages and does not contain violent, suggestive, or commercial content. Because we do not collect personal identifying information, we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

As JapMala evolves, we may update this policy to reflect new capabilities (such as optional encrypted cloud backups or multi-device sync). If any future release introduces external services or modifications to data handling, this policy will be promptly updated with clear explanations before such features become active.

We encourage devotees to periodically review this page for the latest updates.
